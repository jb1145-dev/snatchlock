Protect Your Phone. Effortlessly.

SnatchLock is a smart, eco-friendly wristband and app combo that locks, tracks, and alerts if your phone is snatched.

Features

  • Instant Phone Lock on Disconnection
  • GPS Tracking with Satellite View
  • Alarm and Camera Activation
  • Eco-friendly, Recycled Materials
  • Police-Ready Incident Log

How It Works

Attach the SnatchLock band to your device's charging port and wear it around your wrist. If the connection is broken without disarming the app, your phone immediately locks and alerts are triggered.
